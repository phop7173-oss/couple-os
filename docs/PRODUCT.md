# Couple OS — Product Specification

## Product

Name: Couple OS

Initial product wedge: Movie Date

Category: Relationship / Couple Experience Platform

## Vision

Couple OS is a private shared space designed to help couples:

- experience meaningful moments together
- plan time together
- understand each other's feelings
- make shared decisions
- support each other
- remember meaningful experiences

The product must feel supportive rather than controlling.

It must never replace the judgment, communication, or responsibility of the people in the relationship.

## Core Philosophy

### Support, not pressure

A relationship should help two people support each other through life.

Couple OS should not create unnecessary pressure, competition, or obligation.

### Understanding, not judgment

Future AI capabilities may help couples identify differences in preferences, communication, or feelings.

They must not make definitive judgments about whether someone is good, bad, toxic, compatible, or incompatible.

### Shared decisions

Both partners should have meaningful participation in important shared decisions.

The default model is 50/50.

A different weighting may only be configured when both partners explicitly agree.

### Privacy

Relationship information is highly personal.

Privacy, consent, transparency, and user control take priority over engagement and monetization.

### Quality over quantity

A small number of excellent features is preferable to a large number of shallow features.

## Target Users

### Primary

- Long-distance couples
- Couples who frequently spend time apart
- Couples who want better shared experiences
- Couples who want to plan dates
- Couples who enjoy watching movies together
- Couples who want a private shared relationship space

### Secondary

Couples who live together but want a dedicated digital space for:

- date planning
- shared experiences
- memories
- relationship understanding

## Initial User Problem

Long-distance couples often want meaningful time together but must coordinate:

- what to do
- when to do it
- what to watch
- how to start together
- how to communicate during the experience
- how to make the experience feel like an actual date

Existing communication platforms provide capabilities such as chat, video, or screen sharing, but they are not designed specifically around the emotional experience of a couple's date.

Couple OS aims to make shared time feel intentional and personal.

## Movie Date

Movie Date is the first major product experience.

The desired experience is:

Two people prepare a movie date, enter the same shared environment, watch together, communicate in real time, react to each other, and feel like they are spending time together.

## Movie Date Flow

### Step 1 — Create

Partner A creates a Movie Date.

Partner A can configure:

- movie source
- date name
- date time
- room appearance
- couple characters
- character positions
- available animations and reactions

### Step 2 — Invite

Partner A sends the Movie Date to Partner B.

Partner B can:

- accept
- decline

### Step 3 — Preparation

Both partners configure their side.

The Movie Date remains pending until the required preparation is complete.

### Step 4 — Ready State

Both partners explicitly become ready.

The movie must not begin until the required shared-ready condition is satisfied.

The interface must clearly show each partner's state.

Example:

Partner A: READY

Partner B: READY

100% READY

Movie may start.

### Step 5 — Start

Either partner may initiate the movie once the shared ready condition is satisfied.

The other partner joins the active session.

### Step 6 — Shared Experience

During the Movie Date:

- movie playback is synchronized
- both partners can communicate
- reactions are available
- cute character animations may be triggered
- shared room state remains synchronized

Important shared events include:

- play
- pause
- seek
- join
- leave
- reconnect
- chat
- reaction
- character action
- date status

### Step 7 — Complete

When the movie date ends, the shared session becomes completed.

Both partners should be able to see that the Movie Date has been completed.

The completed date becomes part of the couple's relationship history.

## Couple Characters

Couple characters are part of the emotional identity of Movie Date.

They should be:

- cute
- subtle
- customizable
- non-distracting

Possible interactions include:

- sitting together
- small movements
- leaning
- hugging
- kissing
- simple reactions

The movie remains the primary visual focus.

Animations must not overwhelm the actual movie experience.

## Movie Content

Couple OS must not become an unauthorized movie-distribution or piracy platform.

The product should support legally permitted movie sources and user-controlled content where technically and legally appropriate.

The shared synchronization and couple experience are the product.

Couple OS must not redistribute copyrighted movies without appropriate authorization.

## Real-Time Requirements

Real-time interaction is a core quality requirement.

The system should maintain authoritative shared state.

It must account for:

- latency
- temporary disconnection
- reconnection
- duplicate events
- stale state
- conflicting actions

The experience should feel immediate under normal network conditions.

## Date Planning

Date Planning is the second major product system.

Both partners can propose dates such as:

- movie night
- restaurant
- gaming
- video call
- trip
- walk
- shared activity
- anniversary activity

A proposed date becomes a valid shared date only after the required confirmation from both partners.

## Shared Date Ranking

Both partners should be able to contribute to ranking upcoming date ideas.

Example:

1. Movie night
2. Dinner
3. Gaming
4. Long call

The system should help couples manage limited time, especially in long-distance relationships.

## Shared Decision Model

Default:

50% Partner A / 50% Partner B

Both partners have equal rights by default.

A couple may mutually agree to another weighting model, such as:

- 60/40
- 70/30
- 80/20

Changing the decision model requires explicit agreement from both partners.

The system must never secretly give one partner greater authority.

## Relationship Intelligence — Future

Relationship Intelligence is a future product area.

Potential capabilities:

- helping users understand differences
- helping users reflect on feelings
- identifying communication patterns
- helping couples discuss difficult topics
- suggesting constructive conversations
- helping couples understand preferences

It must not:

- secretly profile a partner
- expose private information without consent
- diagnose mental health conditions
- declare someone toxic
- declare a couple compatible or incompatible as a definitive judgment
- manipulate users into staying or leaving a relationship

The purpose is to support communication, not replace it.

## Monetization

Trust must be established before aggressive monetization.

Potential future revenue:

- premium couple features
- premium Movie Date experiences
- customization
- advanced planning tools
- premium relationship tools
- useful date, gift, and activity recommendations

Advertising, if introduced, should be limited and contextually relevant.

Potential advertising categories:

- gifts
- date activities
- restaurants
- couple-related products

Avoid irrelevant or intrusive advertising.

## Relationship Model

The intended model is:

One Account
    ↓
One Active Relationship
    ↓
One Authorized Partner

A secure invitation and authorization process is required.

Unauthorized users must never access another couple's shared data.

## MVP

The first MVP focuses on proving the Movie Date experience.

Required capabilities:

- account creation
- authentication
- couple creation
- partner invitation and connection
- Movie Date creation
- Movie Date acceptance
- preparation state
- shared ready state
- Movie Date room
- synchronized session state
- real-time chat
- reactions
- customizable couple characters
- basic character animations
- Movie Date completion

## Explicitly Out of MVP

Do not implement these without explicit product approval:

- personality analysis
- compatibility scoring
- relationship health scoring
- AI conflict resolution
- advanced relationship analytics
- advanced recommendation engine
- gift marketplace
- local business marketplace
- complex decision weighting
- advanced memories
- social feed
- public community

## Success Criteria

MVP success is based on real user behavior rather than feature count.

We need evidence that users can:

1. connect with their partner
2. create a Movie Date
3. prepare together
4. start the shared experience
5. experience reliable synchronization
6. communicate and react
7. enjoy the experience
8. want to use it again

Repeated real-world use is the strongest validation signal.

## Product Quality Standard

Couple OS must feel like a real commercial product.

It must not feel like:

- an AI-generated demo
- a generic AI application
- disconnected features
- a clone of a messaging platform

Every major feature should have:

- clear purpose
- coherent UX
- reliable behavior
- loading states
- empty states
- error states
- appropriate security
- appropriate testing

## Product Principle

Help couples experience, plan, understand, support, and remember their relationship without turning the relationship itself into another source of pressure.
